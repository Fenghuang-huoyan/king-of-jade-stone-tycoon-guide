## Tips grounded in confirmed mechanics

- **Rent is a hard deadline.** One achievement is for having only 0–9 cash left right after paying
  rent — running out of cash before rent is due looks like a real failure state to plan around.
- **Checking a stone under the appraisal lamp before buying is the safer default** — going in blind
  is the riskier, achievement-flavored play style, not the recommended one.
- **A single good stone can support multiple pieces of jewelry.** Before selling a stone raw or
  crafting just one item, check whether it can support 2–4 pieces across different styles.
- **Spreading affection across all 8 characters is explicitly rewarded**, not just focusing on one.
- **Endless Mode rewards a deliberate exit.** Three of its five milestone achievements require
  *choosing* to stop at day 25, 40 or 60 rather than being forced out.

## Unconfirmed

Optimal build/talent combinations, ideal early-game stone-buying budgets, and specific crafting
