## The confirmed mechanic

Per the official store page, judging a raw stone involves three steps: **shine a light** on it,
**scrape the skin**, and **open windows** to reveal the jade quality hidden inside. You can stop
at any point — cash out with what you've confirmed, or keep cutting for more, at the risk of
finding nothing or ruining the stone. The store page's tagline: "one cut can make your fortune,
one cut can wipe you out."

## What the achievements confirm

- A dedicated **appraisal lamp** exists — you can check a stone under it before buying (achievement
  "Truth Under the Lamp" / 灯下才说真话).
- "Windows" are tracked as a percentage of the stone opened — one achievement rewards accepting a
  good offer after opening **less than 30%** of a stone's window.
- Stones can end up **cracked**, with a **repair glue** item to fix them.
- A hard-mode achievement ("Judging by Eye Alone" / 肉眼凡胎) requires clearing a chapter *without*
  the lamp, glue, or a collector's card — implying all three are optional aids.

## Unconfirmed

Exact odds of different jade grades, the cash cost of the lamp or opening a window, and how "skin
scraping" differs from "opening a window" mechanically are not published anywhere official yet.
