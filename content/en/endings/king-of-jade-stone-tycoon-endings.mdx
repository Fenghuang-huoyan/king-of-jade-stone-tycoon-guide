## Two kinds of "ending" are tracked separately

- **Chapter completion**: clearing all 4 markets/chapters in a formal playthrough is its own
  achievement, separate from watching all 4 chapters' stories in full and unlocking CGs from all
  4 chapters.
- **Character endings**: tied to the affection system (see [Customers & Relationships](/customers)),
  a character's "canonical ending" unlocks separately from chapter completion. Achievements exist
  for unlocking 1, 4, and all 8 characters' canonical endings.
- Clearing **Pingzhou Night Market** specifically is what unlocks Endless Mode — a distinct,
  named milestone from finishing the other chapters.

## Unconfirmed

Whether chapter outcomes branch based on choices, how many total distinct endings exist beyond the
8-character-ending count, and whether character endings interact with chapter choices are not
documented anywhere official yet.
