## Source and translation note

The game's achievements are only published in Chinese on Steam's public stats page as of this
writing — there is no official English localization for achievement names yet. The table below
gives the **original Chinese name** (verbatim from Steam) plus a **working English translation**
we made ourselves, marked `Unconfirmed` where the meaning is genuinely ambiguous. If 909Game ships
an official English localization later, this page will switch to the official wording.

## What this tells us about the game

Even without playing, the achievement list confirms real systems the store page only hints at:
an **8-character affection system** with CG rewards and individual character endings, **4 formal
chapters/markets** tied to real Chinese jade-market locations, a haggling/pricing system, a
crack/repair mechanic for damaged stones, and an Endless Mode with concrete day-count milestones
(15 / 30 / 40 / 60 days). See [Customers & Relationships](/customers), [Endings](/endings) and
[Endless Mode](/endless) for the pages built from this same list.
