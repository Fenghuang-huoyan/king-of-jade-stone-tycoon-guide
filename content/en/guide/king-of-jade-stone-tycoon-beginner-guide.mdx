## What the game is

King of Jade: Stone Tycoon is a management sim by developer 909Game, published by lvlv, released
September 11, 2026 on Steam. You run a jade stall: buy raw stones, judge the jade hidden inside
them, craft what you find into jewelry, and sell to a cast of recurring customers — all while rent
keeps coming due. The official store page describes it as blending "jade stone gambling, jewelry
crafting, market-stall trading, and strategic build-making."

## The core loop, first hour

Based on the official description and the game's own achievement list:

1. **Source raw stones from the market.** You start on Old Street with a small stall and limited
   cash.
2. **Judge the stone before you buy or after.** The store page describes this as shining a light on
   the stone, scraping the skin, and "opening windows" to see the quality inside — you can buy a
   stone unexamined (a gamble) or pay to check it first. One of the game's own achievements is for
   buying a stone only after checking it under the appraisal lamp.
3. **Decide whether to cut further or cash out.** The store page's own framing — "one cut can make
   your fortune, one cut can wipe you out" — signals a push-your-luck decision at each stage of
   opening a stone.
4. **Craft what you get into jewelry**, or sell the raw stone directly. The achievement list
   confirms jewelry comes in **6 distinct styles** (one achievement requires displaying all 6 at
   once in your display case).
5. **Sell to customers**, who according to the achievements have individual "affection" levels that
   rise as you deal with them and can unlock character stories and CG art.
6. **Pay rent.** Rent is a recurring pressure mechanic named directly on the store page and
   referenced in achievement names about paying rent on time.

## Beyond Old Street

The store page states you move into "ever more challenging jade markets" after Old Street, "each
one brings its own business cycle, rent pressure, and story to clear." The achievement list names
two in-game markets by their real-world jade-trading namesakes: **腾冲老场 (Tengchong Old Market)**
and **平洲夜市 (Pingzhou Night Market)** — both real jade markets in China that the game appears to
model its locations on. There are **4 markets/chapters** total, per the achievement for clearing
all of them.
